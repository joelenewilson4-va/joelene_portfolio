01 — Profile

About

I am an administrative professional who also manages digital marketing and content for grassroots organisations — applying the same consistency and attention to detail across both, whether maintaining organised systems and records or keeping a content calendar on schedule.

My hands-on experience spans over ten years of typing, document preparation, and general office support, paired with several months of self-taught digital marketing and content writing — built entirely on a zero-budget, results-first basis since January 2026.

For D&D specifically, the role extends well beyond content and campaigns — serving as the association's main point of contact, maintaining its databases, capturing and processing data accurately, and managing all correspondence.

I run ChessChat, a community storytelling site and social presence built around the tagline "Every Player Has a Story," managing promotion and content for two chess organisations. That work has sharpened exactly the skills a busy office or remote team needs: organising information clearly, writing copy that lands, keeping platforms consistent, and following through without being chased.

I am now looking to bring that same reliability to a remote administrative, marketing, or content support role.
